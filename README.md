Customer Perception Towards Retail and Institutional Loans at Shriram Finance

📌 Project Overview

This project presents an analytical study of customer perception towards retail and institutional loan services offered by Shriram Finance.

The study focuses on understanding customer awareness, satisfaction, preferences, perceptions, and the factors that influence customers when selecting loan services.

🎯 Objectives of the Study

- To understand the level of awareness among customers regarding different loan products offered by Shriram Finance.
- To examine customer perception towards retail loan services such as personal loans, vehicle loans, and gold loans.
- To evaluate customer satisfaction regarding interest rates, loan processing time, and service quality.
- To identify the key factors influencing customer perception and loan-selection decisions.
- To compare the perceptions of retail and institutional customers.
- To identify challenges faced by customers while availing loan services.

🔬 Research Methodology

Research Design: Descriptive Research

Data Sources:

- Primary data collected through a structured questionnaire/survey.
- Secondary data collected from company reports, official websites, research articles, journals, and relevant publications.

Sampling Technique: Convenience Sampling

Sample Size: 100 respondents

Data Analysis: Percentages, averages, tables, and graphical representations.

📊 Key Findings

Customer Profile

- 62% of respondents were male and 38% were female.
- The largest age group was 25–35 years, representing 40% of respondents.

Loan Product Awareness

- 90% of respondents were aware of Shriram Finance loan products.
- Friends and relatives were the largest source of awareness at 35%, followed by advertisements at 25% and social media at 20%.

Loan Products

- Vehicle loans: 35%
- Personal loans: 28%
- Gold loans: 20%
- Business loans: 10%
- Institutional loans: 7%

Overall Customer Perception

- 22% rated Shriram Finance as excellent.
- 48% rated it as good.
- Overall, 70% of respondents had a positive perception of the company.

Customer Satisfaction

- 60% of respondents were satisfied or highly satisfied with interest rates.
- 70% were satisfied or highly satisfied with loan processing time.
- 68% were satisfied or highly satisfied with customer service.
- 60% were satisfied or highly satisfied with repayment options.
- Overall customer satisfaction was 70%.

📈 Factors Influencing Loan Selection

The major factors influencing customers when selecting loan services were:

Factor| Percentage
Interest Rate| 34%
Quick Approval| 24%
Service Quality| 18%
Brand Reputation| 14%
Repayment Flexibility| 10%

Interest rate was identified as the most important factor, followed by quick loan approval.

⚠️ Major Challenges Identified

The study identified the following customer challenges:

- High interest rates – 30%
- Lengthy documentation – 25%
- Delayed approval – 18%
- Lack of information – 15%
- Customer service issues – 12%

💡 Suggestions

Based on the study, the following suggestions were made:

- Provide competitive interest rates.
- Reduce paperwork and simplify the loan application process.
- Improve customer awareness through promotions and financial education.
- Enhance employee communication and customer-service skills.
- Improve technology and digital verification.
- Provide flexible repayment options.
- Enhance digital loan and customer-service platforms.
- Maintain transparency regarding interest rates, charges, and loan terms.
- Strengthen the complaint-resolution process.
- Conduct periodic customer-feedback surveys.

🏁 Conclusion

The study indicates that customers generally have a positive perception of Shriram Finance's loan services. Vehicle loans were the most preferred product, while interest rates and quick loan approval were important factors influencing customer decisions.

The findings also indicate that customers were generally satisfied with loan processing time, customer service, and repayment options. However, high interest rates and lengthy documentation were identified as areas requiring improvement.

Overall, improving pricing competitiveness, simplifying documentation, strengthening digital services, and enhancing customer support could further improve customer satisfaction and relationships.
